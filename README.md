## About Me

> *"First, solve the problem. Then, write the code."* — John Johnson

I am a **backend-heavy software engineer** with deep expertise in **distributed systems, microservices, real-time data processing, and AI engineering**. My journey as a Computer Science Engineer from IIIT Hyderabad has been shaped by building systems that are resilient, scalable, and performant at massive scale.

At **Walmart Global Tech**, I architect and operate high-throughput backend platforms handling millions of transactions — spanning **Kafka pipelines**, **Elasticsearch clusters**, **Spring Boot microservices**, and **gRPC** interfaces — solving hard problems around consistency, fault tolerance, observability, and scale.

I've also merged classical backend engineering with modern generative AI — designing and deploying **autonomous AI agents** powered by LLMs, orchestrating multi-step reasoning workflows, and building intelligent automation pipelines that bridge backend systems with modern AI.
